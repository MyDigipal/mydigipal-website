What we do

Bespoke work, without the speech that usually comes with it

We draw and build fitted furniture for homes and shops. Everything is made in our workshop, in Toulouse. We subcontract neither the making nor the fitting.

In practice, that means the person who measures up at your place is the one who follows the job to the end, and you have their number.

  • Bespoke kitchens and bathrooms
  • Bookcases, wardrobes, under-stairs storage
  • Shop and office fit-outs
  • Repair and restoration of existing furniture
  • Solid timber, certified panels, water-based finishes
