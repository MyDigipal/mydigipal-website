How we read your need

Groupe Vinso is opening two sites in 2027 and has to train 140 employees without ever stopping production. The difficulty in this tender is not the training content, which is standard. It is in the scheduling of the sessions around operational constraints.

That is why our proposal starts from the production calendar rather than from the training programme. Anyone can write you the programme. The calendar is where feasibility is decided.

Our approach

Three principles, held from start to finish

  1. Never more than 15 % of a team away at the same time. The schedule is built from your production windows, not from our trainers' availability. It is a constraint we impose on ourselves, and it is contractual.
  2. Short sessions, repeated. Two hours rather than a full day. Progress looks slower on paper and is clearly better in practice, because nothing is forgotten between two sessions.
  3. One named contact across the 14 months. Reachable directly, with no call centre and no change of contact mid-engagement. It is the point we get the most positive feedback on.
